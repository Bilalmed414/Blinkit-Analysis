# 🛒 Blinkit Sales & Customer Analytics (Python Project)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Blinkit sales data to evaluate **sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution**. The goal is to identify key business insights and opportunities to optimize sales performance using Python-based data analysis and visualization.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Blinkit wants to understand:

* Which products perform best
* Which outlets generate highest sales
* Customer satisfaction trends
* Inventory distribution patterns
* Sales performance across locations

This project helps answer these business questions using **data analytics**.

---

# 📂 Dataset Description

The dataset contains information about:

* Item Fat Content
* Item Type
* Item Sales
* Item Rating
* Outlet Size
* Outlet Location
* Outlet Establishment Year
* Outlet Type

This data helps analyze sales and customer behavior.

---

# 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following KPIs were analyzed:

* Total Sales
* Average Sales
* Number of Items
* Average Rating

These KPIs help measure overall business performance.

---

# 📊 Analysis Performed

## 1. Total Sales by Fat Content

* Analyzed Low Fat vs Regular products
* Compared customer preferences
* Identified best performing category

---

## 2. Total Sales by Item Type

* Identified top selling product categories
* Compared sales performance across item types

---

## 3. Fat Content by Outlet for Total Sales

* Compared sales across outlets
* Analyzed product preference by outlet

---

## 4. Total Sales by Outlet Establishment

* Analyzed sales trend by outlet age
* Compared performance of older vs newer outlets

---

## 5. Sales by Outlet Size

* Compared small, medium, and large outlets
* Identified best performing outlet size

---

## 6. Sales by Outlet Location

* Analyzed sales by different locations
* Identified high performing regions

---

# 🔍 Key Insights

* Certain product categories generated higher revenue
* Medium sized outlets showed better performance
* Low fat products performed differently across outlets
* Older outlets generated higher sales
* Customer ratings varied across product categories

---

# 📈 Business Recommendations

* Increase stock of high performing products
* Improve performance of low rated items
* Expand high performing outlet locations
* Optimize outlet size strategy
* Improve customer satisfaction
